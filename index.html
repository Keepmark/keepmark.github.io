Browser ⇄ Android

From browser to phone in one scan

Putting your phone on the sync you already use in the browser used to mean typing the service, the sync ID and the password. Now the browser manager shows a connection QR and the phone reads it: service, sync ID and the sync's key arrive together, ready to review. The password never leaves the browser.

Show the code

In the Keepmark browser manager the Android card says “Connect Keepmark on your phone”: choose “Show connection QR”. If you set a sync lock, enter its password first.

Scan it on Android

Open Keepmark for Android → Connect → Link an existing sync → “Scan QR from browser”. The camera is requested only while you scan, and no image is saved.

Review, then link

The phone shows the service and the abbreviated sync ID before anything is sent, then you choose the direction: server copy, merge, or the phone's copy.

The connection QR carries the sync's encryption key, not your password: anyone with a copy can read and change that sync's bookmarks, so show it only to your own device. The browser erases it after 60 seconds or when the window loses focus, and a captured copy stays usable. Nothing is stored for it, a sync lock in the browser keeps others from showing it, and manual linking is always available. Keepmark for Android 1.0.247 or later is needed to read it.

Why Keepmark

Sync you can trust with your data

Built for people who want their bookmarks private and never want to lose one by surprise.

End-to-end encrypted

PBKDF2-SHA256 and AES-GCM on your device. The service that stores your sync sees only ciphertext.

Compatible with xBrowserSync

Works with the official service, community hosts or your own server, and with the official xBrowserSync apps, both ways.

No silent overwrites

When two devices disagree, you choose: take remote, upload local, or merge, with a preview of the counts first.

Safety backups

An encrypted snapshot is taken automatically before every destructive step. Restore it in one tap.

Duplicate cleanup

Find links saved more than once and remove them in bulk. The last copy of each link is always kept.

Several syncs, one active

Work, home, a shared family sync or a second server: every sync keeps its own service, password and bookmarks, and you switch in a click — optionally behind a short sync-change password.

Keepmark for Android

Your bookmarks in your pocket

A native app for Android 10 and later, built around privacy.

  • Encrypted on the phone too: local database with SQLCipher, credentials in the Android KeyStore.
  • Link a browser by QR: scan the connection code from the Keepmark browser manager; the credentials travel between your own devices, never through the server.
  • Select several items and move or delete them together, with a short Undo.
  • App lock with fingerprint or device PIN, with a relock time you choose.
  • Share to Keepmark from any app: the add form opens already filled in.
  • Fast search, pinned and recently added bookmarks, clickable breadcrumb, folders first.
  • Already saved? Adding a page you have tells you where it is; new folders right from the folder picker.
  • Encrypted backup files that also open in the browser extension.

Keepmark for browsers

Set it and forget it

An extension for Chrome, Edge and Brave (Manifest V3) and for Firefox.

  • Automatic sync of toolbar, menu and other bookmarks, plus a “Sync now” button.
  • Several syncs saved side by side — work, home, family — with one active at a time and switching from the popup or the manager.
  • Link your phone by QR from the manager: no service, sync ID or password to type on the phone.
  • Choose your service from eight public hosts with country, size limit and API version, or from the custom URLs you already use.
  • Toolbar icon that follows your theme and shows at a glance whether you are in sync.
  • Clear conflicts with a merge preview, never resolved behind your back.
  • No access to the pages you visit.

Keepmark Server

Run your own sync service

A drop-in replacement for the xBrowserSync API. Official xBrowserSync apps and Keepmark apps work unchanged. Optional: Keepmark works on any xBrowserSync service.

Same contract

Same endpoints, error codes and MongoDB layout, byte-identical v1.1 responses. No user migration.

Safer writes

Every conditional write is an atomic compare-and-swap: two clients pushing at once can no longer silently lose a sync.

Easy to run

Docker Compose with MongoDB and rolling backups, healthchecks, throttling and sync IDs truncated in every log line.

docker pull keepmark/keepmark-api  ·  Docker Hub  ·  Source and docs
