Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Keepmark syncs your bookmarks, end-to-end encrypted, through an xBrowserSync-compatible sync service that you choose. This page covers Keepmark for Android (com.keepmark.mobile) and Keepmark for browsers (Firefox, Chrome, Edge, Brave).

Summary

  • Keepmark does not collect, sell or share any personal data.
  • There is no analytics, telemetry, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind, and no third-party SDK that does so.
  • Your bookmarks are encrypted on your device before they leave it.
  • Keepmark does not create accounts and does not ask for your name or email.
  • The browser extension never reads or modifies the web pages you visit.

What leaves your device

Only when sync is enabled, Keepmark sends your bookmarks to the sync service you selected (the official xBrowserSync service, a community host or your own server). Before sending, your bookmarks are encrypted on the device with AES-GCM, using a key derived from your password with PBKDF2-SHA256.

The sync service therefore stores only:

  • the encrypted bookmark data (ciphertext it cannot read),
  • a random sync ID,
  • timestamps and version information needed for sync.

Your password and your bookmarks in readable form are never sent anywhere. The developer of Keepmark does not receive any of this data. The sync service you choose is operated by its own provider (or by you), and its own privacy policy applies. As with any server, it can see the IP address your requests come from; Keepmark does not send it any other information about you or your device. Keepmark contacts no other server.

Keepmark for Android: what stays on your device

  • Your bookmarks, in a local database encrypted with SQLCipher.
  • The sync ID and the key derived from your password (never the password itself), in Android secure storage backed by the Android KeyStore.
  • Settings, sync state and a local log of recent events. Sync IDs are truncated and secrets are never written to the log.
  • Encrypted safety backups taken before destructive operations.

Android cloud backup of the app data is disabled. Backup files and logs leave the app only when you export or share them yourself, to a destination you pick. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.

Permissions. Internet and network state: to reach the sync service you choose and to know when you are offline. Biometrics: for the optional app lock with fingerprint or device PIN; your fingerprint is handled by Android and never reaches Keepmark.

Keepmark for browsers: what stays on your device

Keepmark stores the following locally in your browser's extension storage:

  • settings (for example the selected service URL and language),
  • the sync ID and the key derived from your password (never the password itself),
  • sync state and an activity log of recent sync events,
  • up to 5 encrypted safety backups taken before destructive operations.

Backup files are created only when you export a backup, and are saved to your device. Removing the extension deletes the local data.

Permissions. Keepmark asks only for what sync needs: bookmarks, storage, alarms (periodic check for changes), notifications (conflicts, removed sync), downloads (backup export) and, on Chromium-based browsers, offscreen (theme detection for the toolbar icon) and network access to reach the sync service you choose. There are no content scripts and no remote code.

Children

Keepmark is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the public repositories KeepmarkMobile and KeepmarkApp.
